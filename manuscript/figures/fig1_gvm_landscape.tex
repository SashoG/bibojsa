% fig1_gvm_landscape -- daughter wavelengths along the GVM level curves of
% the phase-matchable interactions, as a function of pump wavelength.
% v0.11 (17 Oct 2026): the type-II curves are drawn on BOTH halves of the
% phase-matching surface (oeo = eoo in x-z, eoe = oee in x-y), merged by the
% physical condition -- pump matched to the e wave (k_p' = k_e'), EPM, pump
% matched to the o wave (k_p' = k_o') -- so that each is one continuous
% curve through its frequency-degenerate member; the e-wave and o-wave
% wavelengths are the two arms.  Panel 2 is the x-y plane (new); panel 3
% merges the two type-I interactions.
% v0.12 (20 Oct 2026): redrawn for legibility at full text width.  Natural
% width ~ 5.25 in (Optica \linewidth), so the manuscript includes it at
% scale ~1 and footnotesize text prints at 8 pt (was 7 pt x 0.78 = 5.5 pt).
% Shared y range (0.45-3.25 um) with tick labels on the left panel only;
% legend moved below the panels (4 columns) via legend to name.
% Data: data/gvmcurve_xz_<cond>.csv, gvmcurve_xy_<cond>.csv,
%       gvmcurve_<seq>_<target>.csv (type I).
% Compile standalone:  pdflatex fig1_gvm_landscape.tex  (twice)
% 20 Oct 2026 (physics check): legend reads 'condition / theta_PM' -- the
% condition names apply to type II, the angles to the type-I panel.
